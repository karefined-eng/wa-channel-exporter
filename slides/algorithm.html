07 / Technical Strategy

The infinite-scroll algorithm needs proof, not optimism

CORE EXECUTION LOGIC

01. STABILIZATION & MUTATION

Observe layout changes and require two consecutive stable scans before advancing. This prevents partial captures during WhatsApp Web's asynchronous DOM updates.

02. DETERMINISTIC FINGERPRINTING

Combine Channel identity, normalized timestamps, text hashes, and media metadata to ensure virtualization never creates duplicate records in the final archive.

03. DEDUPLICATION & ORDERING

Maintain a global seen-set while preserving first-seen order. If dates are unknown, records are flagged for manual review rather than silent exclusion.

GUARDRAILS & STATES
MAX THRESHOLDS Capped iterations, wall-clock time, and unchanged-height detection to prevent infinite loops.
USER CONTROL Explicit cancellation support with immediate partial-state preservation.
COMPLETENESS STATES Boundary Reached, No Progress, Timed Out, Canceled, or Partial (Unavailable Updates).
