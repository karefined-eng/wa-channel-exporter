01 / PRODUCT STRATEGY
The product promise must be measurable.
Position the exporter as a user-invoked, local-first Channel archive assistant—not an invisible scraper or a promise of deleted history.
RELEASE GATE
Trustworthy completeness, not feature count.
Every export must explain what was observed, included, excluded, downloaded, and unavailable.
POSITION
Archive the Channel posts WhatsApp Web makes available.
SCOPE
Authorized, user-invoked, local browser session only.
OUTPUT
Versioned JSONL plus an auditable ZIP archive.
CLAIM
No “all assets” language until history traversal is proven.
