Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Introduction

WA Channel Exporter ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles your data.

2. What Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing information.

The WA Channel Exporter extension operates entirely on your local device. The extension only reads the DOM of the active WhatsApp Web channel tab in order to extract messages and media for the purpose of generating your offline backup archive.

3. How Data Is Stored

Any configuration preferences (such as your chosen date ranges for exporting) are stored locally on your device using Chrome's local storage API. This data never leaves your computer.

The final exported `.zip` archive is saved directly to your local computer's Downloads folder. We do not host, store, or have access to any of the generated archives.

4. Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or external API services. All code executes locally inside your browser session.

5. Data Sharing

Because we do not collect any data, no data is shared with or sold to third parties.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the extension, please contact us at: Contact Support.
