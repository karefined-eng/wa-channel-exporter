Privacy Policy

Last verified & updated: September 19, 2026

1. Zero-Data Collection Architecture

We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal data, messages, browsing history, or contact details.

WA Channel Exporter operates strictly as a client-side utility executing locally inside your web browser. The extension parses the DOM of your active WhatsApp Web tab solely to generate your requested offline backup files (HTML, CSV, Markdown, Media).

2. Local Storage and Preferences

Any configuration preferences (such as chosen date ranges or media download toggles) are saved locally using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API. This data never leaves your personal device.

All exported files and ZIP archives are generated in memory and downloaded directly into your system's default Downloads folder. We operate no database or external storage service.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Network Calls

The WA Channel Exporter extension contains zero tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or background telemetry network calls. No user information is shared with, sold to, or accessible by third parties.

4. Open Source Verification

Our commitment to privacy is verifiable. You can review the complete source code on GitHub to verify our local-only execution model.

5. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us via our Support page or open an inquiry on our GitHub repository.
